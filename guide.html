💡 Idealist — setup guide

Record ideas and daily tasks by voice, save them to your own Google Sheet, and get Telegram reminders. Takes about 15 minutes, once.

What you'll end up with
  • An app on your phone's home screen with Daily Task and Idea Generator
  • Your own private Google Sheet that stores everything (nobody else can see it)
  • A Telegram bot that reminds you of pending tasks every 4 hours, plus an optional morning digest

You need: a Google account, the Telegram app, and Chrome on Android. Do Part 1 on a computer if you can, it is much easier.

🔐 Use only one Google account while doing this. If you're signed into several, open a Chrome Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N) and sign in with just the account you want to use. This avoids the most common error.

Part 1 — Your sheet and reminders (on a computer)

1

Make your own copy of the sheet

Click the button, then click Make a copy. Keep the name Idealist.

Copy the Idealist sheet

The copy comes with the Ideas and Tasks tabs and the backend script already inside it.

2

Open the script

In your copy: Extensions → Apps Script. A new tab opens with code.

3

Choose your secret password

At the top of the code, find SECRET and replace the text between the quotes with your own long password (letters and numbers, no spaces). You'll type the same one into the app later.

SECRET: 'my-own-long-secret-123',

Press Ctrl+S to save.

4

Set your time zone

Click the ⚙ Project Settings icon on the left, find Time zone and choose yours.

Reminders are paused 10 PM to 7 AM in this time zone, so it should match where you live.

5

Allow the script to use your sheet

  1. Click the </> Editor icon on the left to go back to the code.
  2. In the toolbar, choose setupSheets in the function dropdown and click ▶ Run.
  3. Click Review permissions and choose your account.
  4. If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced → Go to Idealist (unsafe). It's your own copy, so it is safe.
  5. Click Allow.
6

Create your Telegram bot

  1. Open Telegram, search for @BotFather (blue verified tick) and open it.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. Give it a name (e.g. My Idealist) and a username ending in bot (e.g. sam_idealist_bot).
  4. BotFather replies with a token that looks like 123456789:AAH…. Copy it.
  5. Open your new bot and send it a message, such as hi.
Keep the token private, it is like a password for your bot.
7

Connect the bot

  1. Back in the script, paste the token between the quotes of TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN. Save with Ctrl+S.
  2. Choose findChatId in the dropdown and click ▶ Run.
  3. Open the Execution log at the bottom. It says "Your chat ID is: 123456789". Copy the number.
  4. Paste it between the quotes of TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID and save.
  5. Choose testTelegram and click ▶ Run. You should get a message from your bot in Telegram. ✅
8

Turn on the reminders

  1. Choose createReminderTrigger and click ▶ Run. You now get a reminder every 4 hours with your pending tasks.
  2. Optional: for a morning summary, choose createDigestTrigger and click ▶ Run. Change DIGEST_HOUR at the top first if you want a time other than 8 AM.
9

Publish the backend and copy its URL

  1. Click the blue Deploy button (top right) → New deployment.
  2. Click the ⚙ next to "Select type" and choose Web app.
  3. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.
  4. Click Deploy (approve permissions if asked), then copy the Web app URL. It starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/…/exec.
Send that URL to your own phone (e.g. message it to yourself). Don't share it or your secret with anyone, together they give access to your sheet.

Part 2 — The app (on your phone)

10

Install the app

  1. Open this link in Chrome on Android:
    https://puja25c.github.io/idealist/
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen (or Install app).
11

Connect the app to your sheet

  1. Open Idealist from your home screen. It opens on Settings.
  2. Paste the Web app URL from step 9 and type your Secret from step 3.
  3. Choose your voice language, tap Test connection (you should see "Connected ✔"), then Save settings.
  4. Allow the microphone when asked.

Using it

  • Idea Generator: tap 🎤, speak, tap again to stop, fix any words, then Save idea.
  • Daily Task: dictate the task, pick Get it done by, then Save task.
  • My tasks: see what's due, mark ✓ Done or ↻ Reschedule.
  • My ideas: read your ideas and ↗ Share them on WhatsApp, Telegram, Gmail and more.

If something goes wrong

"Sorry, unable to open the file" when opening Apps Script

You're signed into more than one Google account. Use an Incognito window with just one account, then open the sheet and Extensions → Apps Script again.

setupSheets is missing in the dropdown

Save the script with Ctrl+S and reload the page.

I opened a Google "Help" page instead of the permission screen

You clicked a "Learn more" link. Close it, click ▶ Run again, and click the blue Review permissions button.

No Telegram message arrives

Check that you sent your bot a message first, that the token and chat ID are pasted without spaces, and run testTelegram again to see the error in the Execution log.

The app says "Wrong secret" or "Request failed"

The Secret in the app must exactly match SECRET in the script. Also check the URL ends in /exec.

I changed the script, but the app didn't notice

After editing the code, use Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → Version: New version → Deploy. The URL stays the same.

Voice typing doesn't work

Use Chrome on Android, allow the microphone for the app, and check the voice language in Settings. You can always type instead.

Your ideas and tasks live only in your own Google Sheet. Nobody else, including the person who shared this app with you, can see them.
